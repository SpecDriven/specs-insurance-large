# Expire authorized representatives

**Assigned:** Priya Raman

An adult child added as an authorized representative during a claim stays on
the account indefinitely. We have representatives on file from policies that
cancelled years ago, and no record of who authorized several of them.

- An end date on every authorization, defaulting to a stated period
  (customer/identity/manage-authorized-representatives.feature.md).
- The authorizing party, method, and date captured at the time
  (customer/identity/verify-customer-identity.feature.md).
- A review pass over the existing records, with unverifiable ones removed.
