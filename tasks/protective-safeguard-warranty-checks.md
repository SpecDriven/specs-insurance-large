# Verify protective safeguards are still in place

**Assigned:** Miguel Santos

We write protective safeguard warranties on commercial property and credit the
premium for them, then never confirm the sprinkler system still works. The
first check is after a fire, which is a poor time for it.

- Record the safeguards warranted, with their verification date
  (policy/commercial-property/apply-protective-safeguards.feature.md).
- Re-verification required at renewal, with the credit at risk if it lapses
  (policy/commercial-property/quote-commercial-property.feature.md).
- Loss control survey findings feed the verification rather than sitting in a
  PDF (underwriting/inspections/order-loss-control-survey.feature.md).
