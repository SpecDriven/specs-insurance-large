# Use one depreciation schedule across property claims

**Assigned:** Priya Raman

Adjusters apply depreciation from estimating software defaults, a laminated
card from 2019, or judgement. On identical roof claims we have seen holdback
differ by thousands, which is a complaint waiting to happen.

- A published schedule by item category and age, with the source stated
  (claims/property-claims/apply-depreciation.feature.md).
- Deviations require a reason recorded on the claim.
- Recoverable depreciation release is a defined step, not a phone call
  (claims/property-claims/issue-claim-payment.feature.md).
