# Notice when defense spend passes the budget

**Assigned:** Jonas Berg

Defense counsel budgets are agreed at assignment and then nobody looks until
the file closes. Two matters last year ran to roughly double budget with no
conversation in between.

- Compare billed-to-date against the approved budget on each invoice
  (claims/litigation/manage-litigation-budget.feature.md).
- Alert the claim owner and counsel at defined variance thresholds, with a
  required revised budget above the top one.
- Show budget status alongside suit milestones so the picture is one page
  (claims/litigation/track-suit-milestones.feature.md).
