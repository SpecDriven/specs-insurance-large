# Stop missing bordereau reporting deadlines

**Assigned:** Dana Whitfield

Cession bordereaux go out when the analyst gets to them, and two treaties have
tighter reporting deadlines than the others. We have been late on one of them
in three of the last four quarters.

- Deadlines derived from each treaty's own terms, with reminders
  (reinsurance/treaties/define-reinsurance-treaty.feature.md).
- A pre-submission completeness check on the bordereau
  (reinsurance/cessions/report-cession-bordereau.feature.md).
- Reconcile ceded premium to the ledger before sending
  (reinsurance/cessions/cede-premium-to-treaty.feature.md).
