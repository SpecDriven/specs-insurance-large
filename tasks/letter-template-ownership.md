# Give letter templates an owner and a version

**Assigned:** Dana Whitfield

There are forty-odd letter templates and no record of who approved the current
wording of any of them. Compliance asked which version went out in March and
we could not answer.

- An owner, an effective date, and a version on every template
  (documents/correspondence/manage-letter-templates.feature.md).
- Generated letters record the template version used
  (documents/correspondence/generate-notice-letter.feature.md).
- Retired templates stay readable so old correspondence can be reproduced.
