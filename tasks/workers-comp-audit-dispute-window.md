# Give premium audit disputes a real window

**Assigned:** Priya Raman

An audited employer who disagrees with the payroll classification calls the
agent, who calls us, and somebody eventually reverses it by hand. There is no
recorded dispute, no deadline, and no record of who agreed to what.

- A dispute state on the completed audit, with an opening deadline from the
  audit billing date.
- Revised payroll figures recorded as a new audit result, not an edit over the
  old one (policy/workers-comp/run-premium-audit.feature.md).
- Classification changes flow back so the next term starts correct
  (policy/workers-comp/classify-payroll.feature.md).
