# Stop finding subrogation by hand

**Assigned:** Aisha Bello

Subrogation potential is spotted when an adjuster happens to notice a third
party on the loss description. Files that close clean and fast are exactly the
ones nobody reviews, and those are where recovery leaks.

- Screen every claim for third-party indicators at close, not only at intake
  (claims/subrogation/identify-subrogation-potential.feature.md).
- A referral queue with an owner, rather than an email to the subro inbox.
- Track the deductible portion so the insured gets their share back
  (claims/subrogation/recover-deductible-for-insured.feature.md).
