# Quote Motorcycle Policy

## S1: Basic motorcycle quote @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a prospect provides the motorcycle's make, model, and engine size, and the rider's age and license history
- **When** the prospect requests a quote
- **Then** a six-month premium is quoted

## S2: Safety course discount @v1 [proposed]

![diagram.excalidraw](/api/images/diagram-excalidraw-24838e97.svg)- **Given** the rider completed an approved motorcycle safety course within the past 3 years
- **When** the premium is calculated
- **Then** a 10% safety-course discount is applied
