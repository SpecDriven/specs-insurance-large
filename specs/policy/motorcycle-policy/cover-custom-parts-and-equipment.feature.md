# Cover Custom Parts and Equipment

Optional coverage for aftermarket parts, accessories, and riding gear that
exceed the value included in the base physical damage limit.

## Base policy includes a modest accessory allowance @v1 [published]

- **Given** a physical damage policy that includes $3,000 of custom parts coverage at no charge
- **When** a covered collision damages $2,150 of aftermarket exhaust and bags
- **Then** the loss is paid under the included allowance less the collision deductible

## Parts stolen while removed from the motorcycle @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** saddlebags removed and stored in the insured's garage
- **When** the bags are stolen in a garage burglary
- **Then** the theft is not covered under the motorcycle policy while detached
- **And** the insured is directed to the homeowners contents coverage

## Performance modifications void the coverage @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** the motorcycle has a supercharger installed that was never disclosed
- **When** an engine loss is investigated
- **Then** custom parts coverage is voided for material misrepresentation of the risk
- **And** an SIU referral is opened for the undisclosed modification

## Riding gear damaged in the same accident @v1 [published]

- **Given** an optional riding apparel endorsement with a $1,500 limit
- **When** a helmet and jacket are destroyed in a covered collision
- **Then** the gear is replaced at replacement cost up to $1,500
- **And** no separate deductible applies to the apparel loss

## Scheduled custom parts above the included allowance @v1 [published]

- **Given** an insured with $14,000 of documented chrome, paint, and audio work
- **When** additional custom parts coverage is purchased with receipts and photos on file
- **Then** the scheduled limit is shown on the declarations page
- **And** losses are settled at the scheduled amount without depreciation

| scheduled limit | annual premium | documentation required |
| --------------- | -------------- | ---------------------- |
| $3,000          | included       | none                   |
| $10,000         | $84            | receipts               |
| $25,000         | $196           | receipts and appraisal |
| $50,000         | referral       | independent appraisal  |

## Undocumented modifications are limited to the base allowance @v1 [published]

- **Given** an insured who claims $9,000 in custom work with no receipts or photographs
- **When** the claim is adjusted
- **Then** payment is limited to the included $3,000 allowance
- **And** the insured is advised how to document parts for future terms
