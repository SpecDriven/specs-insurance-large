# Cover Additional Living Expense

Coverage D loss of use on the HO-4 form: the necessary increase in living
costs while the rented unit is uninhabitable after a covered peril.

[Jira:INS-812](https://jira.com/INS-812)

## Boarding costs for a displaced pet @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** the temporary housing does not accept animals
- **When** the insured boards two cats for the displacement period
- **Then** reasonable boarding fees are treated as additional living expense
- **And** receipts are required for each billing period

## Civil authority closes the building @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** the fire marshal bars access to the building after a neighboring unit fire
- **When** the insured is displaced without direct damage to the insured's own unit
- **Then** Coverage D responds for up to 2 weeks under the civil authority provision

## Coverage ends at the shortest time to repair @v1 [published]

- **Given** repairs to the unit are reasonably completed in 9 weeks
- **When** the insured chooses to remain in temporary housing for 14 weeks
- **Then** only the 9 weeks of reasonably required expense is reimbursed
- **And** the insured is notified in writing 10 days before payments end

## Hotel and meals after a kitchen fire @v1 [published]

- **Given** a policy with a $9,000 Coverage D limit
- **When** a covered kitchen fire makes the unit uninhabitable for 6 weeks
- **Then** hotel charges and the increase in meal costs over the household's normal spend are reimbursed
- **And** payments stop once the limit is exhausted or the unit is restored

## Lease termination during displacement @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** the landlord terminates the lease because the unit will be rebuilt
- **When** the insured signs a new lease at a higher monthly rent
- **Then** the rent differential is reimbursed for the remaining repair period only
- **And** the claim is closed once the differential period ends

## Only the increase over normal expense is payable @v1 [published]

- **Given** the household normally spends $410 a month on groceries
- **When** the insured submits $980 in restaurant receipts for the displacement month
- **Then** $570 is reimbursed as the increase in living expense
- **And** the reimbursement calculation is documented in the claim file
