# Cover Loss of Rents

Fair rental value coverage pays the rent the landlord loses while a
covered loss makes the dwelling untenantable, for the time reasonably
required to repair.

## Civil authority order extends coverage for two weeks @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a municipal order barring access to the block after a neighbouring building collapse
- **And** no direct physical damage to the insured dwelling
- **When** the tenant vacates and rent is lost
- **Then** fair rental value is payable for up to 14 consecutive days
- **And** coverage ends when the order is lifted

## Extended period of restoration is capped at 12 months @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a total loss where rebuilding is delayed by a permitting backlog
- **When** the period of restoration reaches 12 months
- **Then** fair rental value payments end regardless of any remaining limit
- **And** the adjuster documents the cap in the claim file

## Fire loss pays rent for the period of restoration @v1 [published]

- **Given** a DP-3 with fair rental value at 20% of a $210,000 dwelling limit
- **And** a kitchen fire rendering the unit untenantable on March 9
- **When** the repair scope establishes a five-month period of restoration
- **Then** the monthly rent of $1,650 is paid for five months, totalling $8,250
- **And** the payment is charged against the $42,000 fair rental value limit

## Payment stops when the unit becomes tenantable @v1 [published]

- **Given** an open fair rental value claim with repairs completed on July 14
- **When** the certificate of occupancy is issued
- **Then** fair rental value payments stop as of July 14
- **And** any overpayment for the balance of the month is recovered from the next indemnity payment

## Rent lost to a tenant default is excluded @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a tenant who stopped paying rent in May without any physical damage to the dwelling
- **When** the landlord claims lost rent
- **Then** the claim is denied as not arising from a covered cause of loss
- **And** the insured is advised that eviction costs are also excluded

## Vacant unit at the date of loss pays nothing @v1 [published]

- **Given** the dwelling was unrented and listed for lease at the date of loss
- **When** fair rental value is claimed
- **Then** no rent loss is payable because no rental income was being received
- **And** the dwelling repair claim proceeds independently
