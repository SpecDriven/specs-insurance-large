# Endorse Homeowners Policy

Mid-term changes to an in-force HO-3: limits, forms, interests, and
exposures. Every endorsement produces a revised declarations page and a
pro-rata premium adjustment.

## Adding a trampoline is refused under current guidelines @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** an endorsement request disclosing an unfenced in-ground trampoline
- **When** the request is underwritten
- **Then** the endorsement is refused
- **And** the insured is given 45 days to remove the hazard or accept a liability exclusion

## Adding a water backup endorsement @v1 [published]

- **Given** a dwelling with a finished basement and a sump pump
- **When** form HO 04 95 is added at a $10,000 limit
- **Then** water backup and sump overflow coverage attaches on the requested date
- **And** a separate $500 deductible applies to that peril

## Home business use triggers a form change @v1 [published]

- **Given** the insured reports operating a licensed daycare from the dwelling
- **When** the exposure is endorsed onto the policy
- **Then** the business pursuits exclusion is confirmed as applying to the daycare
- **And** the insured is referred to a commercial general liability quote
- **And** the personal liability limit is not extended to the daycare operation

## Increasing Coverage A re-rates the dependent limits @v1 [published]

- **Given** an in-force HO-3 with Coverage A of $340,000
- **When** Coverage A is endorsed to $410,000 effective May 12
- **Then** Coverages B, C, and D are recalculated at their filed percentages
- **And** an additional premium of $196 is charged pro-rata for 142 remaining days

## Removing a mortgagee after payoff @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a first mortgagee recorded on the declarations page
- **When** the insured supplies a recorded satisfaction of mortgage
- **Then** the mortgagee is removed effective the date of the satisfaction
- **And** billing is switched from escrow to direct bill at the next installment

## Return premium under $10 is waived @v1 [published]

- **Given** an endorsement producing a return premium of $6.40
- **When** the endorsement is issued
- **Then** no refund is generated and the amount is written off
- **And** the waiver is noted on the revised declarations page
