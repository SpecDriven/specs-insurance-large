# Deliver Driving Feedback

DriveRight shows drivers what the program measured and how it affects their
projected discount (telematics/scoring/apply-telematics-discount.feature.md).
Feedback is coaching, not a rating action.

## Coaching tips target the weakest factor @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a driver whose hard braking factor is the lowest of their five factors
- **When** the digest is assembled
- **Then** the coaching content focuses on following distance and braking
- **And** no more than two tips are included per digest

## Driver opts out of push notifications @v1 [published]

- **Given** a driver who turns off trip notifications in the app
- **When** trips are scored
- **Then** no push notification is sent
- **And** trip detail remains visible in the app and in the monthly summary

## Feedback is suppressed for the week of a reported loss @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a driver with a claim reported 3 days ago (claims/auto-accidents/record-car-accident.feature.md)
- **When** the weekly digest run occurs
- **Then** the digest for that driver is suppressed
- **And** normal delivery resumes the following week

## Feedback never states a premium change @v1 [published]

- **Given** any feedback message generated by the program
- **When** the content is rendered
- **Then** it presents scores, events, and projected bands only
- **And** it does not assert a dollar premium amount for the upcoming term

## Trip summary is available after each drive @v1 [published]

- **Given** a completed and scored trip
- **When** the driver opens the app
- **Then** the trip appears within 30 minutes of its end
- **And** the summary shows distance, duration, and any flagged events with their timestamps

## Weekly digest shows the projected discount @v1 [published]

- **Given** an enrolled driver 6 weeks into a monitoring period
- **When** the weekly digest is sent
- **Then** the digest shows the running composite score and the projected discount band
- **And** the projection is labelled as an estimate until the period closes
