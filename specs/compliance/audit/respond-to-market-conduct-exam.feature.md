# Respond to a Market Conduct Exam

A Department of Insurance market conduct examination samples claim, policy,
and complaint files to test handling practices against statute. Responses run
on a short clock and are coordinated by the compliance office.

## Criticism triggers a corrective action plan @v1 [published]

- **Given** an examiner criticism that 6 of 100 sampled claims exceeded the 15-day acknowledgement standard
- **When** the draft report is received
- **Then** a corrective action plan with owners and dates is drafted within 20 days
- **And** the plan addresses the root cause, not only the sampled files

## Error tolerance is met and no criticism issues @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a sampled population with an error rate of 3% against a 7% tolerance
- **When** the examiners score the sample
- **Then** no criticism is issued for that standard
- **And** the result is recorded in the examination record

## Examination notice creates a coordinated response file @v1 [published]

- **Given** a market conduct examination notice from the Ohio Department covering claim years 2024 and 2025
- **When** the notice is logged
- **Then** an examination record is opened with the examination period and scope
- **And** a compliance coordinator and a legal reviewer are assigned
- **And** a litigation hold is applied to every file in scope

## Privileged material is withheld with a log @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a sampled claim file containing coverage counsel opinion letters
- **When** the production is assembled
- **Then** the privileged documents are withheld
- **And** a privilege log identifying each withheld document is provided

## Restitution is calculated across the affected population @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a confirmed pattern of underpaid total loss settlements missing the state sales tax component
- **When** the remediation population is identified
- **Then** every affected claim in the examination period is recalculated
- **And** restitution with statutory interest is issued to each claimant
- **And** a remediation report is filed with the Department

## Sample request is fulfilled from the claim system @v1 [published]

- **Given** an examiner request for 100 randomly selected first-party auto claims closed in 2025
- **When** the sample is drawn
- **Then** the full claim file including notes, reserves, and correspondence is produced for each
- **And** the selection methodology is documented for the examiners
