# Take Examination Under Oath

Invoking the policy's examination under oath condition on a first-party
claim and handling the insured's cooperation duty.

## EUO is demanded under the policy condition @v1 [published]

- **Given** a first-party theft claim with unresolved SIU indicators
- **When** the EUO is demanded under the policy's duties-after-loss condition
- **Then** a written demand naming the date, time, place, and the documents to be produced is sent
- **And** the demand allows at least 15 days before the scheduled date

## Failure to appear breaches the cooperation clause @v1 [published]

- **Given** two properly noticed examinations the insured did not attend
- **When** the second nonappearance is recorded
- **Then** the file is referred to coverage counsel for a breach-of-condition analysis
- **And** no denial is issued without counsel's written opinion

## Insured's counsel may attend but not instruct @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a represented insured whose attorney attends the examination
- **When** the attorney instructs the insured not to answer a relevance objection
- **Then** the question and the instruction are marked on the record and the examination continues
- **And** the unanswered questions are itemized in the post-EUO memorandum

## Interpreter is provided when needed @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** an insured whose primary language is Vietnamese
- **When** the examination is scheduled
- **Then** a certified interpreter is retained at the carrier's expense
- **And** the interpreter's certification is made part of the record

## Rescheduling on good cause is accommodated @v1 [published]

- **Given** an insured who provides a physician's note conflicting with the scheduled date
- **When** a reschedule is requested 6 days before the examination
- **Then** the examination is rescheduled once without prejudice
- **And** the reschedule and its stated cause are documented

## Transcript review period is offered @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a completed examination transcribed by a certified reporter
- **When** the transcript is produced
- **Then** the insured is given 30 days to review and sign with any corrections on an errata sheet
- **And** the claim decision is not finalized before that period closes or is waived
