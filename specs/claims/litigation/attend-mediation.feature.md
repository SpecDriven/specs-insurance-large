# Attend Mediation

Preparing for, attending, and documenting a mediated settlement conference
on a defended file.

**Assigned:** Aisha Bello

## Authority is granted before the session @v1 [published]

- **Given** a mediation scheduled for October 22 with an evaluation range of $95,000 to $130,000
- **When** the pre-mediation review is completed
- **Then** settlement authority of $130,000 is granted in writing before the session
- **And** the authority letter names the person who may commit the carrier

## Impasse is declared with a mediator's bracket open @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a session ending with the carrier at $85,000 and plaintiff at $225,000
- **When** the mediator proposes a bracket of $110,000 to $150,000
- **Then** the impasse is recorded along with the open bracket and its response deadline

## Insured must attend on a limits-exposed file @v1 [published]

- **Given** a file with evaluated exposure above the policy limits
- **When** the mediation is confirmed
- **Then** the insured is invited to attend in person and the invitation is documented
- **And** the invitation explains the potential for a personal contribution

## Mediation statement exchange deadline @v1 [published]

- **Given** a mediator requiring position statements 10 days before the session
- **When** defense counsel's statement is submitted 3 days before
- **Then** the late submission is recorded as a guideline deviation
- **And** the litigation manager is copied on the correspondence

## Settlement at mediation is memorialized the same day @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a case settled at mediation for $118,000
- **When** the session concludes
- **Then** a binding term sheet signed by all parties is obtained before anyone leaves
- **And** the reserve is moved to the settled amount the same business day

## Structured settlement changes the funding approval @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** an agreement funding $400,000 of a $560,000 settlement through an annuity
- **When** the settlement is staged for payment
- **Then** the annuity quote and the rated-age documentation are attached before funding
- **And** the cost of the annuity, not its payout value, is booked as paid indemnity
