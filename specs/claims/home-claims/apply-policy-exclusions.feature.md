# Apply Property Policy Exclusions

Coverage decisions that turn on the exclusions and limitations of the HO-3
form. Every declination here must name the provision relied upon and be
issued in writing.

## Anti-concurrent causation with wind and flood together @v1 [published]

- **Given** a coastal loss where storm surge and wind both contributed to the dwelling damage
- **When** the anti-concurrent causation language is applied
- **Then** damage attributable to flood is excluded even though wind contributed concurrently
- **And** the wind-caused damage above the water line is adjusted and paid separately

## Business pursuits on the residence premises @v1 [published]

A home daycare operating five days a week is a business pursuit, not an
incidental activity. The adjuster documents the operation, the number of
children, and any license, then applies the business property limitation to
equipment and the business pursuits exclusion to any liability exposure.

* Business personal property on premises is limited to $2,500.
* Liability arising from the daycare is excluded absent a business pursuits endorsement.
* The file is referred to underwriting for an eligibility review at renewal.

## Earth movement excluded absent an endorsement @v1 [published]

- **Given** foundation cracking attributed by an engineer to soil subsidence
- **When** the coverage review is completed
- **Then** the claim is denied under the earth movement exclusion
- **And** the insured is told a separate earthquake endorsement was available

## Intentional loss by one insured voids coverage for that insured @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a fire set deliberately by one of two named insureds on the declarations page
- **When** the intentional acts exclusion is applied under the state's innocent co-insured statute
- **Then** coverage is void as to the insured who caused the loss
- **And** the innocent co-insured's proportionate interest is paid where the statute requires it

## Mold limited to the remediation sublimit @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** mold growth following a covered burst supply line
- **When** the remediation contractor submits a protocol costing $14,000
- **Then** payment is limited to the $10,000 fungi and bacteria remediation sublimit
- **And** the sublimit is confirmed as an annual aggregate, not per occurrence

## Wear and tear separated from a covered sudden loss @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a water heater that failed after 19 years and flooded a finished basement
- **When** the claim is adjusted
- **Then** the cost to replace the water heater itself is excluded as wear and tear
- **And** the resulting water damage to the basement is covered as ensuing loss
