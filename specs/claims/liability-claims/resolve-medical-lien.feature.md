# Resolve Medical Lien

Identifying and clearing hospital, health plan, and governmental liens
before settlement funds are released on a bodily injury exposure.

## Child support arrears intercept the claimant's share @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a state child support enforcement notice served on the carrier for the claimant
- **When** the claimant's net share of $11,200 is calculated
- **Then** the intercept amount is withheld and remitted to the enforcement agency
- **And** the claimant and counsel receive a written accounting of the distribution

## ERISA plan reimbursement is negotiated down @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a self-funded ERISA plan asserting $28,400 in reimbursement
- **And** a settlement that does not make the claimant whole
- **When** a reduction is requested citing the plan's own equitable allocation language
- **Then** the negotiated payoff and the plan's written acceptance are recorded on the exposure

## Hospital lien perfected under state statute is honored @v1 [published]

- **Given** a settlement of $45,000 and a hospital lien recorded with the county clerk for $12,700
- **When** the settlement is staged for payment
- **Then** the lienholder is named as a payee on the indemnity draft
- **And** the release is not exchanged until the lienholder's payoff letter is on file

## Lien total exceeds the settlement amount @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** a $20,000 settlement with verified liens totaling $23,900
- **When** payment is staged
- **Then** the settlement is held and escalated to the claims manager
- **And** a pro-rata allocation proposal is circulated to all lienholders

## Medicare conditional payments require a final demand @v1 [published]

- **Given** a claimant enrolled in Medicare Part A at the time of treatment
- **When** the exposure is reported to the Benefits Coordination and Recovery Center
- **Then** settlement funds are held until the final conditional payment demand is received
- **And** the Section 111 reporting record is created for the quarter of settlement

## Unperfected lien does not bind the carrier @v1 [published]

- **Given** a provider letter asserting a lien with no statutory filing
- **When** the lien is validated against the state recording requirements
- **Then** the assertion is documented but not treated as a payable lien
- **And** claimant's counsel is advised that the provider must be resolved from the claimant's proceeds
