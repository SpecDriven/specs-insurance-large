# Match Refund to Original Payment Method

Routes a return premium back through the channel that funded it, which
keeps card networks and anti-money-laundering controls satisfied.

## Card refund window expiry forces a check @v1 [published]

- **Given** an original card payment settled 220 days ago
- **And** the processor's refund window is 180 days
- **When** the refund is released
- **Then** the refund is issued as a check to the first named insured
- **And** the reason "card refund window expired" is recorded

## Mortgagee-funded premium refunds to the mortgagee @v1 [published]

- **Given** a homeowners policy whose premium was paid from escrow by Third Coast Mortgage
- **When** a mid-term cancellation generates return premium
- **Then** the refund is issued to the mortgagee, not the named insured
- **And** the named insured receives a copy of the refund advice

## Refund to a closed bank account is redirected @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** an ACH refund returned with code R02, account closed
- **When** the return is processed
- **Then** the refund is reissued as a check to the mailing address of record
- **And** the bank account is marked unusable for future refunds

## Refund to the card that funded the premium @v1 [published]

- **Given** a $340 return premium on an account funded entirely by a Visa ending 4417
- **When** the refund is released
- **Then** the refund is issued as a credit to that card
- **And** the insured is told to expect 3 to 5 business days for the credit to appear

## Split-funded account refunds proportionally @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** an account funded by a $400 ACH payment and a $200 card payment
- **When** a $300 refund is released
- **Then** $200 is returned by ACH and $100 is credited to the card, matching the funding ratio
- **And** each leg is posted as a separate refund transaction

## Third-party payer refunds require written authorization @v1 [proposed]

- **Given** premium paid by a premium finance company on behalf of the insured
- **When** return premium is generated by a mid-term cancellation
- **Then** the refund is directed to the premium finance company per the assignment
- **And** any redirection to the insured requires written authorization from the finance company
